### IGTL principle (generation)

Judge filler in context — do not synonym-swap. Prefer a concrete product fact
you already have; if none exists, drop the claim or use `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
Never invent RTP, bonus %, wagering, GEOs, or payout times to sound human.
iGaming clichés (“seamless experience”, “unbeatable bonus”, “something for
everyone”) → cut or replace with a real mechanic/condition from the brief.
